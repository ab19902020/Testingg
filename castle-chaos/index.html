    How to play

    Each round, only the first contestants over the line go through. Everyone else is out. Every show picks five events at random. Survive them all to reach the Show Down, then burst the paper ring on Lord Kazan's golden kart to win.

    Phone

    • Drag on the left side to run
    • JUMP and DIVE buttons on the right
    • Final: drag to drive, hold FIRE
    • Drag on the right side to turn the camera
    • Cam button switches the view

    Keyboard

    • WASD or arrows to run
    • Space jump, Shift dive
    • Final: Space or F to fire
    • C switches the camera, drag the mouse (or Z/X) to turn it
    • Esc quits to the menu

    The events

    • Knock Knock: paper doors, solid doors and mud.
    • Skipping Stones: fake stones sink.
    • Boulder Dash: dodge the boulders, hide behind bunkers.
    • Sumo Gong: get past the sumos and bang the gong.
    • Bridge Ball: cross the wobbly bridge while cannons fire at you.
    • Mushroom Trip: hop across bobbing, sliding and bouncy mushrooms.
    • Honeycomb Maze: find the exit, avoid the sticky honey.
    • Big Balls: bounce across giant red balls.
    • Punch Wall: edge past boxing gloves that punch out of a wall.
    • Sweeper: jump the red arm, stay down for the yellow. Last ones standing go through.
    • Show Down: the go-kart water-cannon final.
