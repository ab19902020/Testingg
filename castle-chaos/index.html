How to play

Each round, only the first contestants over the line go through. Everyone else is out. Survive four events to reach the Show Down, then burst the paper ring on Lord Kazan's golden kart to win.

Phone

  • Drag on the left side to run
  • JUMP and DIVE buttons on the right
  • Final: drag to drive, hold FIRE
  • Cam button switches the view

Keyboard

  • WASD or arrows to run
  • Space jump, Shift dive
  • Final: Space or F to fire
  • C switches the camera
  • Esc quits to the menu

The events

  1. Knock Knock: paper doors, solid doors and mud.
  2. Skipping Stones: fake stones sink.
  3. Boulder Dash: dodge the boulders, hide behind bunkers.
  4. Sumo Gong: get past the sumos and bang the gong.
  5. Show Down: water-cannon go-kart battle.
